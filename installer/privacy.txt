devstack and your privacy

devstack has no telemetry, no account and no ads. It connects to the internet only for these:

- Update check. Once a day, and 30 seconds after it starts, devstack reads https://devstack.holostack.in/latest.json to see whether a newer version exists. The request carries only devstack's version (as "devstack/0.1.0"), and the server sees your IP address, like any download. Turn it off in Settings > Updates: "Check for updates automatically".

- Updates. When you click Update now, devstack downloads the new installer from github.com.

- Servers. When you set up devstack or install a version, it downloads nginx from nginx.org, PHP from downloads.php.net and MySQL from cdn.mysql.com. (If Windows lacks the Microsoft Visual C++ runtime, devstack shows Microsoft's download link; it doesn't fetch it.)

Links in the app, such as Sponsor or the source code, open in your browser only when you click them.

Your sites, databases and settings stay on this computer. The full statement: https://devstack.holostack.in/privacy
